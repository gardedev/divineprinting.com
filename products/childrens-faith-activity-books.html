About This Product

Bring your children’s ministry lessons together in a printed activity book. Coloring pages, puzzles and simple exercises can give children something to work through during a lesson and revisit at home.

You supply the activities, illustrations and cover artwork. Include your church name, program theme and the order of the pages so the book reflects the material your team teaches. This printing service does not include prewritten lessons or activity content.

Tell us your quantity, preferred book size, page count and requested date. Use Project Details for paper, binding or finishing preferences; we review the specifications and availability before confirming a quote.
